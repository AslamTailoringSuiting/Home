01WHO WE ARE

Aslam Tailoring & Suiting Center is a local men's tailoring shop built on a simple idea: people feel the difference when something fits properly.

Serving our community in Daska, Punjab, we bring careful measurements, skilled finishing and thoughtful details to traditional kameez shalwar and modern men's suits alike. Whether you're choosing something dependable for every day or dressing for a memorable occasion, we take the time to get the fit and finish right.
